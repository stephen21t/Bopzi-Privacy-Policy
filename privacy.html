Bopzi Privacy Policy

Developer: MadeByST

Last updated: 15 October 2026

MadeByST develops and operates Bopzi, a free arcade game for Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Bopzi.

1. Information We Collect

Bopzi does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address or other personal information to play.

Game information, such as your personal best score and preferences, may be stored locally on your device to support gameplay.

2. Advertising

MadeByST plans to introduce optional rewarded advertisements using Google AdMob.

When advertising is enabled, Google's advertising services may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, app interactions and diagnostic information to deliver, measure and protect advertisements.

Advertising-related data practices are described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps and services here: Google's partner information page

3. How Information Is Used

Information handled by Bopzi is used to support game functionality, remember game preferences and maintain the player experience.

Once advertising is integrated, information may also be processed by advertising services for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and related purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Bopzi may use third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services operate under their own privacy policies and may process information as described above.

5. Children's Privacy

Bopzi does not require players to provide personal information directly to MadeByST.

If Bopzi is made available to children, MadeByST will review the applicable Google Play Families requirements and configure advertising and data handling accordingly.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game information may be removed by clearing Bopzi's app data or uninstalling the game, depending on device settings and backup behaviour.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own privacy policies. For privacy enquiries or deletion requests, contact MadeByST using the email below.

7. Data Security

MadeByST aims to use reasonable measures to protect information handled by Bopzi. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Bopzi introduces new features, advertising services or changes to its data practices. Updates will be published on this page.
